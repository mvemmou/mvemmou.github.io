I'm a computer architect who works at the boundary of hardware and systems software — designing more expressive interfaces between compute, memory, and networking to make datacenter systems faster and more adaptable. My dissertation, Toward Adaptive Modular Computing Systems Through More Expressive and Elastic Hardware and Software Interfaces, was advised by Assoc. Prof. Alexandros Daglis at Georgia Tech. I'm broadly interested in CXL and disaggregated memory hierarchies, and in datacenter energy efficiency.

I consider teaching and advising an integral part of my academic life — see Experience and Service for more on that side of my work.
