I served as Student & Faculty Affairs Chair. I strongly believe in collective action and cooperation between graduate students, faculty, and administration to create an enjoyable and equitable graduate student experience — we worked with the SCS administration to improve student well-being, including increasing stipends, organizing social, academic, and career events, mentoring new students, and participating in new faculty recruitment. The PhD years are immensely demanding, and often isolating. Fostering solidarity and kinship between students, and cultivating avenues of communication and mutual respect with faculty and administration, is paramount.
