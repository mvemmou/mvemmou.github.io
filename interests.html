Outside the Lab

Clothing and Costume — I sew and knit, and read a lot about the history, sociology and psychology of clothing.

Reading — Both fiction and non-fiction; The Bell Jar and One Hundred Years of Solitude are long-time favorites.

Football — a devoted fan, courtesy of my father.
